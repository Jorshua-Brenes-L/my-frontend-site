SeaTracker

Marine species tracking device

Device Operation

Sea Tracker is an innovative device designed for real-time monitoring of marine species, allowing precise and detailed biometric information to be obtained about each specimen. This advanced system collects data such as approximate size and weight, common and scientific name, depth at which it is found, and behavioral patterns such as hunting activity or displacement. Its operation is based on a small sensor that is carefully placed on the species, without affecting its well-being, and transmits the data to a digital platform for analysis, conservation, and scientific research. With Sea Tracker, marine research reaches a new level of precision and sustainability, offering a key tool to protect oceanic biodiversity and better understand life under the sea.
